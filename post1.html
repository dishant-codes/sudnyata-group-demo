Waigaon Turmeric: The Golden Heirloom

A treasure from Maharashtra, known for its purity and medicinal properties.

A Legacy Rooted in Tradition

Deep in the heart of Maharashtra, the farmers of Waigaon have nurtured a special variety of turmeric for generations. Free from chemicals and full of natural potency, this turmeric is hand-harvested and sun-dried, preserving its golden brilliance.

What Makes Waigaon Turmeric Special?

  • High Curcumin Content: Contains up to 7% curcumin, making it more potent than regular turmeric.
  • Rich Aroma & Taste: A strong, earthy fragrance with a deeper flavor profile.
  • Powerful Medicinal Benefits: Used in Ayurveda for its anti-inflammatory and healing properties.
  • 100% Organic: Grown using traditional methods without artificial chemicals.

Health & Beauty Benefits

  • Boosts immunity and fights infections.
  • Promotes digestion and gut health.
  • Reduces inflammation and joint pain.
  • Brightens skin and fights acne.

Waigaon Turmeric vs. Regular Turmeric

Normal Turmeric

  • Lower curcumin content (2-4%).
  • Mild aroma, slightly bitter taste.
  • Grown with chemical fertilizers.
  • More affordable, widely available.

Waigaon Turmeric

  • High curcumin content (6-7%).
  • Strong aroma, richer taste.
  • 100% organic, traditionally grown.
  • Premium quality, rare to find.

Recognized for Its Excellence

Waigaon Turmeric received the prestigious Geographical Indication (GI) Tag in 2016, marking it as an authentic, high-quality spice.

A Step Towards Sustainable Living

Choosing Waigaon Turmeric is about more than just premium quality. It’s about supporting Indian farmers, promoting sustainable agriculture, and making a healthier choice for your family.
